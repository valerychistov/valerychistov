## About me

I'm building learning resources and interactive web tools around **digital transformation** and **strategic foresight**. My current projects explore how technology reshapes organizations, business models, and industries, with an emphasis on making complex ideas easier to understand and apply.

I'm especially interested in:

- **Technology and organizational change:** connecting digital tools with strategy, culture, and value creation.
- **Possible futures:** combining emerging trends to explore how industries could evolve.
- **Interactive learning:** bringing course materials, visual frameworks, and scenario exercises together on the web.

## Selected projects

### [Disruptive Foresight](https://github.com/valerychistov/disruptiveforesight)

A digital transformation course hub with an online course book and an interactive scenario workspace.

**Foresight Studio** lets you combine three trends with an industry to generate Gemini-assisted scenarios for five- and ten-year horizons. It supports structured exploration of plausible strategic futures.

`HTML` · `CSS` · `JavaScript` · `Gemini API` · `GitHub Actions`

[Explore the repository →](https://github.com/valerychistov/disruptiveforesight)
